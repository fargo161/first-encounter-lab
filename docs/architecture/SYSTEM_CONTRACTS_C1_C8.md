# Proposed system contracts C1–C8

Proposed for owner review. These describe handoffs and proof obligations. Exact functions and schemas depend on later authorized implementation. Producer, exact package or artifact, local kind/ID, native version, and any frame should travel together. Encounter owns gameplay history. ROOM supplies geometry and physical evidence, Atlas reads descriptive projections, and presentation reads committed cues.

**Customer purchase.** A4 supplies a correlated arrival at a shelf, and A1 commits a legitimate price observation. A2 freezes the Pay-for-Give terms sent by A6 or A3. A3 considers the clerk's view. A1 records exact assent, checks execution and uniquely records payment and then handover. If handover fails, the saved run exposes the outstanding effect, a distinct retry or an explicit refund. Any learned receipt or lasting judgment has its own cause. A5 performs the committed cues, after which reload must leave the ledger unchanged.

**Hold and return.** A2 freezes the Keep request and payment undertaking. A counter names the chosen hold terms or a defer names a fact and follow-up. After owner approval of the policy, A1 records each owed part and any deposit/reservation separately. The logical clock runs catch-up once on return. Only eligible observers learn relevant events. A1 validates the outstanding payment and handover or release, preserving overdue history and any late fulfillment. Saved obligations, actor bindings, and earned references survive the visit.
