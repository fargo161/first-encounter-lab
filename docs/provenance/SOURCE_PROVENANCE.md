## Candidate overlay inventory

Preflight §E reports 951 originals, 38 additions, six replacements, and zero removals for a total of 989 assembled scratch files. The two source replacements were `source/ui-route.js` (nine hook changes) and `source/build.py` (includes the new `source/ui-lab.js`). Four other replacements were generated/test outputs, `Room_Creator.html`, `Play_Combat_Room.html`, `tests/characters_data_results.json`, and `tests/route/out/char_shots/01_start.png`. Additions include the boxed store scene and generator, mannequin builder/packs, physical add-on, play preview and author tests. The checkpoint's Claude report §2 and handoff §§5–8 explain each. The read-only preflight scratch diff covers all 44 changes.

The useful pieces are the authored layout, body and face artifacts, scene probes, and an editor-based physical preview. The directed route API permits unsafe paths and has uncorrelated arrivals, a resettable event buffer and unresolved camera/restore. Generated IDs changed in 22 leaves when rebuilding the Lab scene. Any future candidate must pin the scene or make IDs reproducible. See [preflight §E](../preflight/FIRST_ENCOUNTER_LAB_PREFLIGHT_REPORT.md#e-claude-checkpoint-reuse-ledger) and [M01](../decisions/OPEN_APPROVALS.md#m01-room-interface-and-source-ownership).
